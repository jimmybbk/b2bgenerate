Privacy Policy

How B2BGenerate collects, uses and protects information from website visitors, inquiry contacts and clients.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

B2BGenerate LLC ("B2BGenerate", "we", "us") is a performance-based cold email agency registered in Sheridan, Wyoming, USA, serving business clients worldwide. Our operating hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Indochina Time (ICT).

You can reach us on WhatsApp at 0450 463 767, or through the form on our contact page.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers information we handle through this website, including form submissions, email and WhatsApp inquiries, and general use of the site. Information about prospects contacted within client campaigns, and client campaign data generally, is governed by the service agreement between B2BGenerate and the client.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Form submissions: your name, work email address, company name, website, WhatsApp number, the service you are interested in, and a description of who you sell to, submitted through our contact forms.
  • Messages: the content of any message you send us by email, WhatsApp or the footer contact form.
  • Call or meeting details: if we schedule a strategy call, the name, contact details and scheduling information you provide.

Information collected automatically

  • This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Our site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and delivery purposes, under GitHub's own privacy terms.
  • If we add analytics tooling in future, we will update this policy before doing so.

4. How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare the campaign plan you requested.
  • To provide, manage and report on our services.
  • To schedule and hold strategy calls.
  • To improve our website and service content.
  • To comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not build advertising profiles.

5. Legal bases (where GDPR or UK GDPR applies)

  • Consent: when you voluntarily submit an inquiry form or message us, you consent to us using those details to reply.
  • Contract: to perform services you have engaged us for.
  • Legitimate interests: responding to business inquiries, securing the website, and keeping business records.
  • Legal obligation: retaining records where law requires.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate: our email provider for receiving inquiries, a form-delivery service (FormSubmit) that forwards website form submissions to our mailbox, our web host (GitHub/Microsoft), and WhatsApp/Meta if you contact us there. These providers process data under their own privacy terms.
  • Professional advisers (accountants, lawyers) where reasonably required.
  • Authorities where the law requires or to protect our rights.

7. International transfers

We operate internationally and your information may be processed in the United States, Thailand and other countries where we or our providers work. Where local law requires, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as contractual clauses or an adequacy framework.

8. Retention

Inquiry and form data is kept as long as needed to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterwards (typically up to 24 months), unless you ask us to delete it sooner or you become a client, in which case service data is retained per the service agreement and applicable law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Residents of some jurisdictions (including the EEA/UK, California and others) also have rights to know what personal information is collected and to request deletion, and we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise any right, contact us using the details in section 16. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law.

10. Marketing messages

We do not add inquirers to newsletters or mailing lists. Form submissions are treated as one-to-one business correspondence. Where we send commercial electronic messages, we identify ourselves clearly, include functioning opt-out instructions, and honor opt-outs promptly, in line with anti-spam frameworks such as CAN-SPAM (US), GDPR/PECR (EU/UK), CASL (Canada) and Australia's Spam Act 2003.

11. Cookies

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting-level technical processes necessary to deliver the pages you request are handled by GitHub Pages.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, including HTTPS delivery and limiting access to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children

Our services are business-to-business and not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

14. Third-party links

Our site links to third-party properties (such as WhatsApp). We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please review their policies separately.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. The "last updated" date at the top shows the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

16. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: Wayne Clothier, B2BGenerate LLC, Sheridan, Wyoming, USA. Message WhatsApp 0450 463 767 or use the form on our contact page. We respond within one business day where practical.

Note: This policy describes our current practices as of the date above. It is provided for general information about this website and does not constitute legal advice.
