B2B Marketing Consultants: What They Do, What They Cost and How to Choose the Right One

B2BGenerate · Guides

If they come and go in unpredictable waves - hectic one month, quiet the next - then perhaps a B2B marketing consult would do it (the word 'consultant' can describe people from strategists who never see a campaign through to people who will build and run your whole outbound engine, but either one that is a stretch for your budget will be an expensive lesson).

Here is a handy guide for B2B marketing consultants, their fee rates (for 2026) and how to find one who builds your pipeline, not just your PPT.

What Are B2B Marketing Consultants?

Unlike consumers, B2B sales focus on winning over business customers. For complex B2B sales, there are often between six to ten decision makers within the buying organisation to consider (a buying group) and only 17% of buyer time during the purchase is spent in evaluation and negotiation with potential suppliers , 77% describe their most recent purchase as complex or challenging to complete (Gartner 2009).

However, marketing to them is not the same as selling to the end consumer; there are different factors that need to be understood to develop an effective strategy. In a B2B environment, the sales process can be lengthy, high value deals have many stakeholders (Procurement, Finance & the end user), all of whom have different criteria (payback, workflow & risk for examples amongst others) that they will be looking to influence.

What Does a Consultant Actually Do?

These five functions describe the role of the individual on a daily basis.

1. Plan your B2B marketing - This is really about auditing what you have (which channels are adding to the bottom-line and which are draining the cash-flow) and then doing an assessment to get an idea of the competitive landscape, setting a realistic, revenue-linked objective for the campaign and developing the plan itself around all of that.

2 Identifying target organisations and decision-makers.

"Companies that might need us" is not a target market but the consultant will have developed customer profiles: sectors, size of organisations, revenue, geography - even the decision makers, the influencers and the blockers. So the cleaning company may have different messaging for the facilities manager when calling on office blocks, hospitals or warehouses.

3. Develops lead generation tactics. This is your strategy in action! Whether it is cold email outreach, LinkedIn campaigns, SEO, Google Ads, referral programs, etc. It will be tailored around your prospect, but not just because it is 'cool'.

4. Marketing and sales are no longer siloed In B2B the single biggest leak in the sales pipeline is from the handover from your lead being sent to them taking any meaningful sales action. Sales are now defining what constitutes a quality (and therefore worth pursuing) lead. Gartner sales survey, recently, found that 61% of B2B buyers actually feel a rep is no longer needed at all, so your marketing has a lot of selling to do before that appointment goes in.

Measuring cost to acquire a new customer - cost per lead, conversion rates on all stages etc. If they tell you the cost of a new opportunity and it is not a number, then they are telling you about effort, not result.

The Services Most Consultants Offer

B2B marketing consultants are often involved in a mix of the following areas:

  • Lead generation - creating repeatable systems to create qualified enquiries not just isolated hits.
  • So whilst B2B Email Marketing is still the workhorse in B2B. With 66% of marketers claiming email to be their number one channel for lead generation. It is the classic, for every $1 of a dollar you invest, $36 will be returned as a result for example.
  • Search engine optimisation - keyword research, technical fixes and landing pages (those researching alone and using search engines will come to your site)
  • Content (e.g. guides, case studies, white papers - the research phase of the journey).
  • Account-based marketing - targeted campaigns against a shortlist of key accounts (than the whole market); 87% of B2B marketers surveyed by ITSMA say that ABM offers higher ROI than other marketing activities
  • - Paid advertising (Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads if the targeting strategy ( intent or job-title) justifies the media spend)

Start by defining your market and your competition, determine the characteristics of an ideal customer and then decide which channels to use. From there it is a matter of execution and improvement over time. Your choice of channel should reflect what you are trying to achieve.

What Does a B2B Marketing Consultant Cost?

As in Consulting Success' survey of just under 1,000 consultants, there is a wide range in pricing options: 30% charge on a task basis, 29% charge an hourly rate, 16% monthly retainers, 15% value-based, and 10% charge day rates.

As a broad and unscientific estimation, for the year 2026, the ballpark for these sorts of companies are that the independent professionals charge about $75-$250 an hour with the more experts at higher rates than this range. Then there are monthly retainers which would usually be of the order of $1,500 through to $15,000 with more defined projects being anything from about $1,500 for a single task audit type project right up to anything in excess of $50k for the whole go-to-market process. They generally will not include advertising, lead generation, software usage etc. into these fees either.

And remember - you need to think value per qualified opportunity; if a consultant is a low cost but produces nothing, this may end up being the most expensive in the market.

Consultant or Agency: Which Do You Need?

The truth is consultants tend to be strategy / advisory based while agencies offer delivery and are usually comprised of a small team of media buyers, deliverability engineers and copywriters. It is never one or the other though. If you have internal marketers who need advice then hiring a consultant can amplify their efforts. If however you want the job doing then the agency (or, indeed, consultant that can deliver) is best. So never afraid to ask honestly what sort of company they are.

When Hiring One Makes Sense

One of the hardest things to do is getting traffic and generating leads. HubSpot have been doing marketing research for years now and this issue always ranks the highest for marketers with 61% saying it is the biggest challenge they are facing with their business. When should you call in someone who knows? If your leads are being sporadic, your campaigns are being done but are underperforming or just because your business is now going into a new area or you do not have the skills but cannot employ them fast enough or you are relying too much on referrals but it is taking you out of your comfort zone and you need a systematic acquisition process.

How to Choose the Right One

Seven things to check before you sign anything:

So what characteristics should you look for in partners and agencies? Just because they can market to you or an end-user, it does not mean they understand working with a buying decision group of six. Before recommending anything to you, their questions should go deep into the dynamics of your buyer. What specific media do they intend to use and to whom and when. Do not focus on how many people saw ads and how you felt; get data (replies, qualified opportunities and meetings). No magical formula that will guarantee instant search engine rankings and sales. Credible partners will spend equal time on process before talking about profit. There should always be someone who can be contacted and regular reviews with KPIs. Just because they have a bargain price does not mean it is the cheapest path to your full pipeline.

Questions to Ask Before You Sign

So far, who have you managed to work with who are clients in our industry? What is your ideal customer profile (or, what would be)? Where should we focus our advertising efforts and what will you explain why? How would you go about measuring quality - not volume - of leads? What sort of reports will you be giving us and how often? How long is your timeline and are you including costs for advertising, database development and software within your quote?

Why Lead Generation Sits at the Centre of It All

Because B2B buyers are doing increasing amounts of work to understand before calling on suppliers, most B2B success or failure will live or die in outbound prospecting. Here are two stats about prospecting which are always worth understanding: 1) Prospectors will typically have to work multiple times before they get their first meeting so on average it takes 8 touches before the first meeting takes place. 2) whilst on any email your statistics are small numbers the total adds up, so Martal B2B benchmarks are about 42% open rate, 4.8% reply and about 1.1% conversion into booked meetings for cold campaigns whilst not high in absolute numbers the quality of the responses is still of much more importance

Without an expert, such discussion will be speculation. A wise expert will factor in such matters from the outset.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Trying to reach everyone at once, chasing volumes above value, not focussing on what happens after the hand-off to sales, using the same message to the same list of leads (in both cases the same 'one size fits all' approach), measuring vanity metrics that look good but add little real value, having the expectation that a pay campaign will deliver you the same results as an SEO campaign - and then wondering where your sales leads have gone. All of this can be avoided if there is a clearly thought out plan and someone who will be open transparent about their actions and performance. The problem is, who is the one hiring?

The Bottom Line

Effective B2B marketing is not more funds, it is about insight into the market and a focused value proposition and repeatable process to translate unknowns to qualified opportunities and customers. Know what is missing and match the expertise to your needs and requirements when selecting a provider.
