Our story

Built by people who were tired of the same old story

B2BGenerate was founded in Sheridan, Wyoming by B2B operators who kept seeing the same story: bloated retainers, offshore list-blasting and "activity reports" with nothing in the calendar to show for it.

So we built the agency we wanted to hire. A small senior team that owns your entire outbound channel, agrees on what a "qualified meeting" means before a single email is sent, and reports on the only numbers that matter: replies and booked meetings.

  • Plain-English reporting: no vanity metrics, no jargon
  • Senior strategists on every account, never junior handoffs
  • Compliance built in from day one, not bolted on after
  • A simple performance promise: if we don't produce leads, you don't pay
